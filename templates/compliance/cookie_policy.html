1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text data files stored on your local browser by web applications. They allow websites to remember your login session, prevent cross-site request forgery attacks, and persist your display preferences.

2. Cookies Used by IIMT UNIVERSITY

IIMT UNIVERSITY categorizes its cookies into three clear levels. We never use third-party behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookie Name Category Duration Purpose & Description
sessionid Essential 2 Weeks Maintains authenticated user session across secure academic dashboard views.
csrftoken Essential 1 Year Cryptographic protection against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) form attacks.
IIMT UNIVERSITY_cookie_consent_v1 Functional 1 Year (localStorage) Stores your authorized cookie choices so you are not prompted repeatedly.

3. How to Control Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to our interactive preference manager, you can delete or block cookies directly through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: Disabling Strictly Necessary cookies (`sessionid` or `csrftoken`) will prevent you from logging into your IIMT UNIVERSITY dashboard.
