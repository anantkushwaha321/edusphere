Academic Tuition & Fee Refund Policy

Effective: {{ last_updated }} • Compliance: Federal Title IV & State Higher Education Standards

1. Commitment to Transparent Fee Structures

IIMT UNIVERSITY is dedicated to total transparency in academic billing. We pledge:

  • Zero Hidden Fees: No undisclosed technology maintenance surcharges, late submission penalties, or opaque processing fees are ever imposed.
  • Upfront Disclosures: All course credit costs, lab equipment supplies, and syllabus material requirements are published in the course catalog prior to registration.
  • Direct Disbursement: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment without administrative deduction penalties.

2. Semester Withdrawal & Tuition Refund Schedule

When a student drops a course or withdraws from the academic term, tuition refunds are calculated systematically according to the institutional calendar:

Withdrawal Date Range Tuition Refund % Lab / Course Material Fee Transcript Impact
Prior to Official Semester Start Date 100% Refund 100% Refund No Record on Transcript
Day 1 to Day 14 of Semester 80% Refund Pro-rata (Unopened Kits) Course Dropped (No Grade)
Day 15 to Day 30 of Semester 50% Refund Non-Refundable Noted as Withdrawn ("W")
After Day 30 of Semester 0% (Incurred Load) Non-Refundable Formal Grade Evaluated

3. Exceptional Extenuating Circumstances

In cases of serious medical emergency, military mobilization, or severe family bereavement documented by competent authorities, the Academic Registrar grants pro-rata exceptions beyond standard deadlines. Petitions should be directed to the Bursar's Office.

4. Processing Timelines & Contacts

Refund disbursements are processed within 7 to 10 business days following formal registrar approval. Inquiries regarding billing status should be directed to:
