1. Our Commitment

IIMT UNIVERSITY is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for students, faculty, and administrators with disabilities. We continuously refine the user experience to ensure equal access, adhering to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards and Section 508 of the US Rehabilitation Act.

2. Implemented Accessibility Measures

High Color Contrast

All text content meets or exceeds the required 4.5:1 contrast ratio against background elements (3:1 for large headings).

Full Keyboard Navigation

All interactive components (navigation menus, modal dialogs, form controls, buttons) are fully navigable via standard Tab, Enter, and Esc keys.

Screen Reader Optimization

Semantic HTML5 landmarks (`

`, `
`, `
`, `
`), descriptive `alt` tags on photos, and `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative icons.

Skip-to-Content Mechanism

A direct bypass link allowing keyboard and screen-reader users to jump directly to the primary page content without navigating sidebar links.

3. Assistive Technology Compatibility

IIMT UNIVERSITY is tested and compatible with modern assistive tools including:

  • NVDA and JAWS screen readers on Windows.
  • Apple VoiceOver on macOS and iOS.
  • Native browser zoom scaling up to 200% without loss of layout integrity or text clipping.
