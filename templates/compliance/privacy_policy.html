2. Principle of Data Minimization

Under Article 5(1)(c) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and FERPA guidelines, IIMT UNIVERSITY operates under strict data minimization standards. We only collect and process personal data that is adequate, relevant, and strictly limited to what is necessary in relation to academic instruction, assignment evaluation, attendance certification, and institutional management.

  • We do not collect sensitive biometric data, financial payment numbers, or social media identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal student or faculty data to any third party.
  • We do not employ automated behavioral tracking or cross-context behavioral ad surveillance.

3. Categories of Personal Data Processed

Data Category Purpose & Lawful Basis Retention Period
Account Credentials & Profile (Name, Email, Roll Number) Authentication, role provisioning, and academic identity (Contractual necessity). Duration of academic enrollment + 5 years for transcript verification.
Course Work, Submissions & Marks Academic instruction, grading, and accreditation review (FERPA / Legitimate Interest). Retained per institutional grading archive mandates (7 years).
Attendance Records Statutory exam eligibility certification & compliance oversight. Duration of academic degree program.
Security Audit Logs (IP addresses, Timestamps) System security, fraud prevention, and session integrity (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Rolling 90-day window, then permanently sanitized.

4. Protection of Minors (COPPA & GDPR-K)

IIMT UNIVERSITY is designed in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8. For any enrolled student under 13 years of age (or under 16 within the European Economic Area):

  • Verifiable parental or legal guardian consent must be recorded prior to activating access to online academic submissions.
  • Guardians maintain the statutory right to review, request deletion, or revoke consent for their child's educational records at any time.
  • Guardians can access our dedicated Verifiable Parental Consent Portal.

5. Audit of Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure

IIMT UNIVERSITY has conducted a strict vendor assessment to exclude invasive tracking SDKs. We do not integrate tracking beacons, Meta pixels, or third-party ad networks. Our minimal external dependencies include:

  • PostgreSQL Database Engine: Self-hosted/private encrypted database instance at rest (AES-256).
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDN): Tailwind CSS and Chart.js served strictly for UI rendering without telemetry tracking.

6. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You hold the following enforceable rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access (Art. 15) Inspect and receive confirmation of all personal records held in the database.
Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) Download your complete academic records in structured JSON format via our self-service portal.
Right to Erasure / Deletion (Art. 17) Request complete erasure of non-mandatory personal data.
Right to Rectification (Art. 16) Correct inaccurate profile records or typographical errors anytime.
